India customs & bags — read this first

  • Alcohol: 2 litres of alcoholic liquor or wine per adult passenger arriving by air, duty-free. This is current under India’s Baggage Rules, 2026 (in force 2 Feb 2026). Two 70cl bottles = 1.4L (safe). A 1L Baileys plus a 70cl whisky = 1.7L (still under). Do not go over 2L unless you will declare and pay duty.
  • Andhra Pradesh: AP is not a dry state. Duty-free alcohol cleared at the airport is for personal use only — do not sell it. Keep the receipt and original packaging. Some relatives may not drink; take chocolates and dry fruits as the default “everyone” gifts.
  • Goods allowance: for an Indian resident / person of Indian origin arriving by air, the 2026 free allowance for other goods is ₹75,000 (this replaced the older ₹50,000 figure). Used personal effects you reasonably need are separate. One new laptop is also allowed for passengers 18+. Over the limit → red channel and duty.
  • Airline bags: liquids over 100ml must go in checked baggage (UK security). Weigh tins, nuts, oil and bottles — they add up fast. Typical long-haul is 23kg per checked bag (check your ticket).
  • OK: chocolates, nuts, tea/coffee, vitamins for personal/family use (keep original packs). Don’t pack: meat or meat products, fresh food, or extra gold/silver beyond jewellery limits.
  • Plugs: India is 230V (fine). Wall plugs are Type C/D, not UK Type G — pack a small adaptor, or prefer USB-C gadgets.
  • Confirm before you fly: CBIC. This is a trip checklist, not legal advice.
